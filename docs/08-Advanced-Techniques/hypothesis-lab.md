# Hypothesis Lab / Research Ops

## Purpose

A personal R&D track for novel classes — separate from daily bounty grind.

## Cadence

| Block | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| 70% | Production hunting on programs |
| 20% | Deep dive one technology (e.g. a gateway) |
| 10% | Write-ups / notes back into BugLibrary PRs |

## Lab rules

- Prefer local intentionally vulnerable apps for dangerous classes  
- Production BB: only safe slices of novel research  
- Record negative results (what did *not* work)  

## FR

70% bounty, 20% recherche ciblée, 10% documentation. Lab local pour le dangereux.

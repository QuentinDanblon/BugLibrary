# Data Handling

## Principles

- **Minimum necessary** data for proof  
- Prefer your own test records  
- Redact tokens, passwords, session IDs  
- Hash or truncate real user identifiers if accidentally encountered — and stop  
- Delete raw dumps you do not need  

## Evidence retention

Keep enough to defend your report; avoid hoarding customer PII.

## FR

Minimum vital pour la preuve. Rédiger secrets. Ne pas stocker de PII clients inutilement.

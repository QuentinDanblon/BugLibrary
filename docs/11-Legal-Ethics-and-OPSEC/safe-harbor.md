# Safe Harbor Realities

## What safe harbor usually requires

- Good faith research  
- Compliance with program rules  
- No privacy violations / data destruction  
- Prompt reporting  
- No extortion  

## What it does **not** do

- Legalize attacks on out-of-scope systems  
- Override criminal law everywhere automatically  
- Protect reckless DoS  

## Practical advice

Read the **exact** safe harbor text per program. When in doubt, stop and ask.

## FR

Safe harbor = bonne foi + respect des règles. Pas un permis hors scope. Lire le texte exact.

# Professional Ethics

## Non-negotiables

1. No illegal access outside authorization  
2. Minimize harm and data access  
3. No extortion — ever  
4. No selling unfixed bugs to third parties when under program terms forbidding it  
5. Respect user privacy of real customers  
6. Honest severity and originality  

## Good citizenship

- Report quickly when impact is high  
- Avoid public 0-day drops for clout  
- Credit co-finders  
- Mentor without gatekeeping  

## FR

Pas d’extorsion, minimiser les données, honnêteté de sévérité, divulgation responsable.

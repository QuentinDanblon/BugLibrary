# Mission & Safety Contract

<!-- updated: 2026-03 -->

## English

### Mission

Build and maintain the **highest-signal** open reference for authorized bug hunting: methodologies that win bounties, techniques that reflect 2024–2026 reality, and reporting patterns that survive triage.

### What success looks like

- A new advanced hunter can ship a **valid, high-clarity** first report within weeks, not months of flailing
- An AI agent can load section indexes and execute **scoped** workflows without inventing out-of-scope attacks
- Teams share a **common language** for severity, impact, and OPSEC

### Explicit non-goals

| Non-goal | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Weaponized exploit kits | Legal & ethical risk; fails platform norms |
| Illegal access tutorials | Criminal assistance is out of scope forever |
| Infinite CVE encyclopedia | Structure + depth beat unmaintainable breadth |
| Replacing program policy | Program rules always win |

### Safety contract (read this)

1. **Authorization first** — bug bounty, VDP, or signed RoE only  
2. **Minimize harm** — no DoS “for fun”, no data exfil beyond proof, no account takeover of real users  
3. **Private disclosure** — follow platform disclosure timelines; never drop 0-days “for clout”  
4. **No secrets in reports/repos** — redacted tokens, hashed IDs when needed  
5. **AI agents inherit human liability** — the operator owns every request  

### Relationship to law

BugLibrary documents **how professionals think and work under authorization**. It does not grant permission. When in doubt: stop, re-read scope, ask the program.

## Français

### Mission

Maintenir la référence open-source au **meilleur signal** pour le bug hunting autorisé : méthodologies qui gagnent des bounties, techniques 2024–2026, reporting qui passe le triage.

### Contrat de sécurité

1. **Autorisation d’abord**  
2. **Minimiser le dommage**  
3. **Divulgation privée / responsable**  
4. **Pas de secrets** dans rapports ou dépôts  
5. **L’opérateur est responsable des agents IA**  

### Non-objectifs

Pas de kits d’exploit weaponisés, pas de guides d’accès illégal, pas d’encyclopédie CVE infinie au détriment de la maintenabilité.

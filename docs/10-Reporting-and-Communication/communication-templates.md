# Communication Templates

## Request retest

```text
Hi team — thanks for the fix notification.
I retested on <date> with accounts <A/B>.
Result: <fixed / still reproducible>.
If fixed: steps now correctly denied at step <n>.
If not: updated evidence attached.
```

## Clarify N/A

```text
Thanks for the review. I believe there may be a misunderstanding on <point>.
Policy reference: <quote/section>.
Additional evidence: <minimal>.
Could you please re-evaluate? Happy to jump on any needed detail.
```

## Self-close / withdraw

```text
After further testing I agree impact is limited / intended.
Please close. Thank you for your time.
```
